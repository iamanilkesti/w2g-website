Estate creation is the process of systematically building assets and financial resources that can support your own goals and eventually form part of the wealth you pass on to your beneficiaries.
Estate planning becomes relevant as soon as you begin accumulating meaningful assets or have people financially dependent on you. It should be reviewed as your family, assets and responsibilities change.
Nomination is an important operational step, but it may not replace a complete succession plan. The legal treatment can vary by asset and jurisdiction, so wills, nominations and ownership structures should be reviewed with qualified legal professionals.
