What We Look At

Understand not only what you own, but how the different parts of your financial portfolio work together.

Asset Allocation

Review how investments are distributed across equity, debt, fixed income, cash and other relevant asset classes.

Diversification & Overlap

Identify concentration and duplicated exposure and assess whether diversification is meaningful.

Risk Review

Assess portfolio risk in relation to objectives, investment horizon and financial circumstances.

Performance Review

Look beyond headline returns and understand performance in the context of category, risk and holding period.

Goal Alignment

Connect investments with retirement, children's future, wealth creation and other planned milestones.

Liquidity & Protection

Consider emergency liquidity and protection needs alongside long-term investments.

Why Review?

Owning Investments Is Not the Same as Having a Portfolio Strategy.

Over time, investments can accumulate across funds, policies, deposits and accounts. Market movements and changing life goals can also alter the portfolio you originally intended to build.

Understand where your money is currently invested.
Identify unnecessary concentration or investment overlap.
Check whether your portfolio still reflects your goals.
Discuss areas that may need review or rebalancing.
A Better Question

Not just “How much return did I make?”

A useful review asks whether the portfolio is taking appropriate risk, remains diversified, has sufficient liquidity and is progressing toward the purpose for which the money was invested.

Our Approach

How the Portfolio Review Works

A structured process designed to turn scattered investment information into a clearer financial picture.
